AlexStudio / Darkroom session

The darkroom.

Before screens, every print Alex made started here: a negative, an enlarger, three trays and a red lamp. Make one yourself.

  1. 01 Negative
  2. 02 Expose
  3. 03 Develop
  4. 04 Fix & dry

Choose a negative

Hold the strip up to the lamp. Light and dark are reversed: what is bright here prints black.

Expose under the enlarger

Pick an aperture, switch the lamp on, count, switch it off. While it burns, move your hand (pointer or finger) over the paper to dodge: hold back light and that area stays lighter.

Aperture
00.0 s Alex's test strip says f/8 for about 6 seconds.

Rock the tray

The image comes up out of white paper, shadows first. Drag across the tray to agitate: fresh developer works faster. Pull it when the blacks are rich and the whites still clean.
