Orange + rosemary

The bitter sweet spot.

Bring these

  • A chilled Bitter Orange
  • Plenty of ice
  • An orange wheel + a rosemary sprig

Make it yours

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
  2. Pour Bitter Orange slowly over the ice.
  3. Add the orange wheel, brush the rosemary between your palms and tuck it in.
