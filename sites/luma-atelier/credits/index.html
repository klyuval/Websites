About the imagery

A world imagined.
Sources, credited.

The sample collection combines generated editorial imagery with credited photographs. These images do not represent client commissions photographed by Alex.

The About Alex page uses an AI-edited portrait and three generated scenes of Alex working on land, in a hot-air balloon and on a boat. These illustrate the settings he has worked in; they are not photographs of actual past assignments.

The portrait poster uses an AI-edited likeness of Alex. Its studio background and material close-ups were generated as illustrations, not photographs of his premises or completed commissions.

The Working Studio is an illustrative studio, not Alex’s premises or client work. Six shots were generated with Kling 3.0 Pro through the direct Higgsfield API and edited into dedicated landscape and portrait films.

The four editorial images below, camera artwork, and calla-lily, negative-film ribbon, frame-material specimen and light-meter details were created with the built-in ChatGPT image generator. The desktop camera film was animated with Seedance 2.5 using the direct Higgsfield API. Frame previews are illustrative; final materials are agreed with Alex.

Ten additional photographs were generated with the built-in ChatGPT image generator for events, portraiture, restoration, framing and print materials. They are illustrative studies, not client commissions. The Light Room film plays independently of scrolling.
